Key Concepts (Digital Communication Systems 1)

1. Encryption Secures digital data before transmission by applying mathematical algorithms (e.g., XOR cipher) to hide plain text contents.
2. Line Coding Converts binary bits (0s and 1s) into voltage waveforms suitable for the physical channel.
• NRZ-L: Level shift.
• Manchester: Mid-bit transition for synchronization.
3. Channel Noise & BER Noise corrupts signal amplitude in the channel. When noise exceeds decision thresholds, bit errors occur, affecting the BER metric.
